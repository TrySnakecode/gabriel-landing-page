Frequently asked questions

Before we build,
let's talk.

A few answers to help you get started with confidence.

Do I need all my copy and images ready?+

Not necessarily. We can determine what content is needed during planning and identify the materials you already have. Producing or licensing new assets should be part of the agreed scope.

Do you build mobile-friendly websites?+

Yes. The mobile experience is designed from the start, not improvised after the desktop version.

How does pricing work?+

Pricing depends on scope, page count, content, integrations, and interactivity. An initial conversation helps define the right solution before preparing a proposal.

Can the website be updated later?+

Yes, but content editing and future updates need to be agreed upon in the project scope. We can choose the structure that best suits your needs.

Are the portfolio websites real client projects?+

No. Bruma, Alva, Prumo, and Risco are fictional brands and concept projects. They demonstrate design decisions and navigable experiences, not commissioned work or proven business outcomes.
