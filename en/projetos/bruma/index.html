01 / Overview

An editorial experience about architecture, materials, and taking a moment to pause. The website invites visitors to explore conceptual work at their own pace, highlighting imagery, light, and negative space.

The challenge

Create the feeling of visiting an architecture studio without turning navigation into a visual maze.

The idea

Organic Luxury: understated styling, immersive photography, and typography that gives architecture room to breathe.

bruma.

02 / Visual language

An identity with its own voice.

Typography, color, and proportion shape each brand's positioning. Each of the four projects has its own distinct visual language.

Typography — Cormorant Garamond + DM Sans

Information hierarchy makes it easy to discover the studio, compare concepts, and explore three fictional projects. Slow-paced transitions support the contemplative atmosphere.

Design decisions

An experience that gives architecture room to breathe.

Bruma explores how an interface can become a quiet frame around its content: few actions, precise hierarchy, and photography as storytelling.

01

Information architecture

Projects take center stage, with clear paths to individual work, the studio, services, and contact.

02

Editorial rhythm

Cormorant Garamond typography, generous spacing, and a balance between light backgrounds and large-scale imagery.

03

Meaningful interactions

Room exploration, a material library, and illustrative lighting comparisons. No effects just for decoration.

04

Honest concept presentation

Reference photographs are not completed works by the fictional brand. Interactive interfaces simulate the architectural presentation, not a built project.
