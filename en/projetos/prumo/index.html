01 / Overview

A business-focused experience designed for decision-making. Prumo turns a facilities and maintenance service catalog into a clear journey: understand, choose, and describe the need.

The challenge

Organize technical services and industries so that different audiences can quickly find relevant solutions.

The idea

Premium business design featuring deep navy, amber accents, and a clear content architecture.

Design decisions

Organization that makes decisions easier.

Prumo demonstrates how a technical services company can present its solutions clearly, help visitors find the right services, and organize information for a quote.

01

Task-oriented information

Content grouped by service type and target audience, so visitors don't need to search through generic pages.

02

Clear paths to a quote

Contextual calls to action throughout the website, instead of hiding the only action in the footer.

03

Adaptive form

Fields adapt to urgency and service type, with a review before the demo confirmation.

04

Trust without invented numbers

The project does not invent clients, contracts, certifications, or teams.
